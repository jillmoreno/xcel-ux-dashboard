Two conflicts between the sources

The wireframe brief and the build plan disagree about the website.

The wireframe brief says plainly: "Website and checkout are out of scope for now — a separate brief." The build plan then lists self-serve purchase and signed-in browsing among the ten Phase 1 acceptance journeys, and both run through the website. So two of the ten things that define "Phase 1 is finished" sit in a surface we have been told not to design. Those rows are marked Needs info rather than guessed at.

Nobody has settled whether the admin dashboard is ours.

The build plan records four contested surfaces: "Compass PRDs still assign B2B admin, Insights dashboards and Signals/Analytics to Compass. Our architecture assigns Admin, the analytics surface, Support and Notifications to FinServ with Compass supplying signals only. Left unreconciled, both teams build admin." The wireframe brief says the same thing from the design side — "do not design an admin dashboard that assumes either answer."

This is not a Compass row we can park. It decides whether a large share of the Admin & Analytics inventory below is work we do at all, and it is the single most consequential unresolved item across all three documents.
