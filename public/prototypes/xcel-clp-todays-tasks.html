Today's Tasks — nine readings of the same panel

The Current Learning Path card's right-hand panel currently ends in Up next: one line naming the chapter after this one. That line answers what comes after, which is a question nobody is asking while they are logged in. Today's Tasks answers what am I here to do — but the moment a task can be overdue, the panel has two jobs, and the variants below differ in how much room they are willing to give the second one. Switch the day state above; every variant re-renders.

Round 1 (baseline, A–E) asks how much of the panel overdue is allowed to take. Round 2 (D1–D3) develops the one that won — D, the week calendar — by throwing away the calendar it invented and rebuilding it in the Study Calendar's own vocabulary, with the tasks in a right-hand column. All three round-2 variants share one week model, so the only thing being compared is what the calendar is for.
