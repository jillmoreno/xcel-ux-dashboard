01How to read this map

Compass Learning is one surface of the product with three versions behind one switch: Current (what ships today, Anjani's), Jeff's MVP (his thirteen-page prototype, ported page for page), and Refined (Readiness rebuilt on the November research, with the other pages forked from Jeff's). All three live in the same build and are picked on the Design controls bar, so a reviewer looking at any one of them is one dropdown away from the other two — and nothing on the page says which one they are on, except the bar's label.

This map goes page by page, and on each page block by block, top to bottom. For every block it records:

RowWhat it tells you
WhereThe block's position on the page, in reading order.
To see itThe address, the bar selection, and any click needed once you are there — "expand the Annuities row", "open the levels panel". If the row says nothing extra, the block is on screen the moment the page opens.
CopyThe words on screen, verbatim. Numbers that are typed into the template rather than computed are called out, because they are the ones that will not change when the data does.
DataThe fixture and field the block draws from, for whoever has to change it.
StatesAny way the block can look other than the way the screenshot shows it — open or closed, present or absent.
Design controlsAny flag on the Design bar that changes this block, with its variants. Most blocks on Jeff's pages say none: his version is one authored state, and that is deliberate.
Vocabulary

The Design controls bar is the light-cyan bar under the dark review bar. It starts hidden; open it from the robot's menu (UI/UX Demo Tools). Its first label names the surface you are on — Compass · Jeff's MVP, Compass · Refined — and that label is the only place the version is named on screen. The amber Demo controls bar above it is about the learner, not the design, and nothing on it picks a Compass version.

02Opening Jeff's version

Jeff's version is a Compass section of the dashboard, not a route of its own. The shortest way in is the address; the bar is how you get there from anywhere else.

By address — lands on Readiness directly
Address/dashboard-rebrand?demo=1&section=compass&ff=compass-mvp-version:jeff&compassPage=readiness
Piecessection=compass opens Compass · ff=compass-mvp-version:jeff pins his version · compassPage=readiness opens on that page instead of Overview. Drop the last one for his Overview.
By the bars — from any Compass page
1Open the robot's menu and show the Design controls bar.
2Set Compass version to Jeff's MVP. The bar's surface label changes to Compass · Jeff's MVP. The control is scoped to the e2e family of dashboard versions and Jill/Atlas-Main; on the default version it is there.
3In his left rail, under EXAM PREP, click Readiness. It is the last row.
Design controls→Compass version: Jeff's MVP→Rail · Exam prep→Readiness
What the switch does and does not change

The Compass version control decides which version renders — and on Jeff's Readiness page that is the only flag that matters. No other design control touches a block on it. The same control at Refined opens on Readiness by default and adds one more control (the hero's ring treatment), which is why the Refined column, when it comes, will have more in its Design controls rows than this one.

03Readiness — Jeff's MVP mapped

His Readiness page answers one question — what should I do next? — and puts the answer at the top before it shows anything about how it is going. Ten blocks, top to bottom. One authored learner throughout: 62%, Developing, Annuities weakest. There is no empty state and no before-the-first-exam state; the page has one shape.

Files. src/components/learning/CompassJeffMvpReadiness.tsx renders it inside CompassJeffMvpShell.tsx; the data is JEFF_MVP_READINESS and the copy JEFF_MVP_READINESS_COPY, both in src/data/compassJeffMvpFixtures.ts; styles in compassJeffMvp.css, prefix cjm-.
