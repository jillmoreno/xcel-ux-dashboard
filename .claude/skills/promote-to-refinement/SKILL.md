# Skill: Promote to Refinement → `share`

**This skill was renamed `share` on 2026-10-15** (Jillienne: *"can we make
promote to refinement an easier command, like share"*).

**Read `.claude/skills/share/SKILL.md` and follow it.** There is nothing else
here on purpose: two copies is how a step gets fixed in one and not the other,
which is the same reasoning that makes `netlify/lib/linkBoard.ts` one factory
for four boards rather than four hand-copied functions.

The name is kept because it is written down in places that outlive a rename —
the Contributing guide, `docs/gateway.md`, `promote-to-prototype`'s closing
step, and three designers' muscle memory. `/promote-to-refinement` still works
and always will; `/share` is shorter.
